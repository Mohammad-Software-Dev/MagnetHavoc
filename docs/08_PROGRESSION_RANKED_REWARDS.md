# Progression, Ranked, and Rewards

## Progression layers

Magnet Havoc should separate **skill ranking**, **account progression**, and **cosmetic collection** so players understand what each system represents.

### Account level
Long-term XP track earned from playing. Unlocks onboarding features, currencies, and cosmetic rewards. Never increases combat stats.

### Ranked rating
Measures competitive performance. Visible league tiers communicate status.

Suggested leagues:
- Bronze
- Silver
- Gold
- Platinum
- Diamond
- Master
- Havoc Legend

Use divisions within early tiers if needed for pacing.

### Seasonal track
A free and premium Havoc Pass provides goals and cosmetics during a season.

### Mastery
Optional later system tracking skill/use milestones such as Core steals, knockouts, successful prop hits, or wins in a mode. Rewards are cosmetic/titles.

## Ranked principles

- No paid stat advantage.
- No hidden “engagement matchmaking” designed to manipulate purchase behavior.
- MMR should prioritize fair matches and reasonable latency.
- Rank display can be more forgiving than underlying MMR, but logic must be coherent.
- Parties should be matched against similar party structures when population allows.

## Rating model

Use a proven rating approach such as Elo-like or Glicko-style hidden MMR, adapted for free-for-all placement if Core Rush is ranked.

For FFA:
- Placement and opponent strength matter.
- Raw knockouts should not outweigh objective success.
- Leaving early counts as a loss unless server fault is detected.

For team modes:
- Team outcome is primary.
- Personal stats should have little or no direct rating impact to avoid selfish play.

## Seasons

Recommended starting cadence: 6–8 weeks.

Season contents:
- Ranked soft reset.
- One headline cosmetic theme.
- New/rotated arena modifier or event.
- Havoc Pass.
- Limited challenges.
- Leaderboard snapshot/rewards.

Do not require a new gameplay system every season.

## Daily/weekly goals

Goals should encourage variety without forcing bad behavior.

Good examples:
- Play 3 matches.
- Win with the Core for 20 cumulative seconds.
- Land 10 Push hits.
- Play one match with a friend.
- Complete two different modes.

Bad examples:
- Throw matches to farm a weird action.
- Force use of a paid cosmetic.
- Require excessive session length.

## Reward philosophy

The player should earn something frequently but major cosmetics remain aspirational.

Reward categories:
- Bolts (soft currency).
- Pass XP.
- Cosmetic items.
- Profile titles/banners.
- Limited seasonal tokens, only if absolutely necessary.

Avoid currency proliferation.

## Comeback and loss treatment

Even a loss should provide:
- Basic account XP.
- Progress on reasonable challenges.
- Clear performance feedback.
- Immediate rematch option.

Do not punish losing so strongly that players avoid ranked play.
