# Marketing and Positioning

## Market-facing promise

**Push them. Pull them. Throw everything.**

Magnet Havoc should be presented as instantly understandable physical competition, not as a deep sci-fi lore game.

## Store pitch

Short version:

> Run, dash, pull the Core, launch objects, and blast rivals off the arena in fast multiplayer magnetic mayhem.

## Differentiating hooks

- Fight using magnetism instead of guns.
- The arena itself becomes your weapon.
- Short 2-minute matches.
- Physics creates funny unscripted moments.
- Easy controls; competitive mastery.
- Play solo, with friends, or in teams.

## Creative concepts

### 1. Reverse the attack
Rival throws metal crate → player Pulls it past them → Pushes it back → rival flies off map.

### 2. Last-second steal
Leader has 98 score → challenger yanks Core away → dashes → reaches 100 first.

### 3. Friend betrayal
Two friends appear to cooperate → one Pushes the other off the edge → reaction/emote.

### 4. Impossible recovery
Player is launched toward void → last-second dash lands on moving platform → immediate counter-Push.

### 5. Chain reaction
Prop hits player → player hits bumper → bumper knocks Core loose → another player catches it.

Each ad should show real or faithfully representative gameplay. Avoid misleading fake mechanics.

## App icon direction

- One expressive Mag close to camera.
- Visible magnet effect or opposing polarity.
- One rival/prop flying backward.
- Strong silhouette at tiny size.
- Avoid tiny text.

## Logo direction

“MAGNET” readable and mechanical; “HAVOC” more energetic. Consider subtle opposing-pole motif. Must work monochrome and at small horizontal sizes.

## Tagline options

- Attract. Repel. Survive.
- Push. Pull. Prevail.
- Turn the arena against them.
- Pull the Core. Push your luck.
- Physics fights back.

## Community content

Encourage:
- Knockout clips.
- Trick shots.
- Friend challenges.
- Ranked promotion moments.
- Custom match chaos.

Do not rely on creator virality as a substitute for paid acquisition testing and retention quality.

## Naming note

“Magnet Havoc” is a working/product name and still requires formal trademark, app-store, domain, and regional clearance before commercial commitment.
