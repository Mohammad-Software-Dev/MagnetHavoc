# Art, Animation, VFX, and Audio Direction

## Visual identity

**Stylized industrial sci-fi sport.** The world is energetic, clean, and playful rather than dystopian. Characters should read as collectible toys brought to life in a futuristic arena league.

Key adjectives:
- Chunky.
- Magnetic.
- Kinetic.
- Bright.
- Mechanical.
- Playful.
- Competitive.

Avoid photorealism. Mobile readability and production efficiency matter more than material complexity.

## Shape language

Players:
- Rounded, compact body mass.
- Oversized magnetic hands/emitters or visible chest coil.
- Clear facing direction.
- Large expressive visor/eyes.

Arena:
- Large simple architectural shapes.
- Strong edge silhouettes.
- Mechanical props with obvious mass.
- Hazard components visually distinct from decorative machinery.

Core:
- Unique orb/contained-energy silhouette.
- High contrast.
- Never confused with paid VFX.

## Color language

Exact palette is not locked, but semantic roles are:
- Pull: cool polarity family.
- Push: warm/opposite polarity family.
- Objective Core: unique neutral/energy highlight distinct from team colors.
- Hazard: standardized warning treatment.
- Friendly/enemy team accents: accessibility-tested alternatives.

Do not rely on color alone; pair with shape, icon, and motion.

## Animation style

Characters should exaggerate physical reactions:
- Lean during acceleration.
- Feet/hover system scramble when resisting Pull.
- Body compresses before dash.
- Push recoils the torso/arms.
- Pull creates visible strain.
- Heavy impacts blend into tumble.
- Recovery has a quick spring-up quality.

Keep gameplay hitboxes stable despite visual exaggeration.

## VFX priorities

### Push
- Expanding directional wave.
- Brief polarity symbol or ring.
- Impact streaks aligned with force direction.

### Pull
- Curved field lines/particles moving toward player.
- Target outline/pulse when affected.
- Increasing intensity as object nears lock range.

### Core possession
- Distinct trail.
- Small scoring pulse.
- Holder marker readable through clutter.

### Knockout
- Strong but short burst.
- Character-specific cosmetic effect can play without obscuring nearby gameplay.

## Audio identity

Magnetism needs a recognizable sound vocabulary:
- Pull: rising electric hum + metallic resonance.
- Push: compact bassy “whump” plus high-frequency snap.
- Target lock: short click/chirp.
- Prop impact: mass-dependent clang/thud.
- Dash: air/electric burst.
- Core pickup: rewarding tone distinct from currency sounds.
- Final 10 seconds: escalating music/percussion without masking effects.

## Music

Music should support a televised futuristic sport:
- Energetic electronic percussion.
- Short match intro sting.
- Dynamic layer during Overload/final seconds.
- Strong victory/defeat stingers.

## Performance constraints

- Prefer baked/simple lighting appropriate to mobile.
- Keep transparent particle overdraw under control.
- Pool VFX.
- LOD or simplified mesh variants as needed.
- Avoid excessive real-time shadows on low-end devices.
- Provide quality tiers.

## Accessibility

- Screen shake slider/off.
- Haptics toggle.
- Color-blind-safe team/objective options.
- Subtitles/text cues for critical announcer messages.
- Avoid essential information conveyed only through sound.
