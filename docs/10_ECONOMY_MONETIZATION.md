# Economy and Monetization

## Business principle

Magnet Havoc is free-to-play and competitive. Monetization should sell **identity, collection, celebration, and convenience around cosmetics**, not combat power.

## Currency model

Keep the economy simple.

### Bolts — soft currency
Earned through play, account progression, challenges, pass rewards, and events.
Used for selected common cosmetics or cosmetic rotations.

### Prisms — premium currency
Purchased with real money and occasionally granted in small amounts through special rewards.
Used for premium cosmetics and premium pass access.

Avoid adding additional permanent currencies unless a specific system truly needs one.

## Store categories

- Featured skins.
- Emotes.
- Victory animations.
- Magnet VFX.
- Bundles.
- Havoc Pass.

Store presentation should show cosmetic previews in motion because animation/VFX are central to value.

## Havoc Pass

Seasonal pass with free and premium tracks.

Premium rewards may include:
- Themed skin.
- Magnet effect.
- Victory animation.
- Emotes.
- Prisms/Bolts.
- Profile banner/title.

Pass progression should be achievable through ordinary play rather than narrow chores.

## Ads

Recommended philosophy: restrained.
- No forced interstitial during a ranked match flow.
- No rewarded ad that grants combat power.
- If ads are used, place them in optional non-competitive contexts such as a limited bonus reward or cosmetic trial.
- Measure whether ad friction harms retention before scaling.

The game should be commercially viable through IAP if possible; ads are supplementary, not the core experience.

## Pricing

Exact price points are intentionally unspecified in this design package. Use regional pricing tiers, player research, and soft-launch data. Do not hardcode money values into gameplay code.

## Fair-play red lines

Never sell:
- Stronger Push.
- Faster movement.
- Extra Flux capacity in ranked.
- Shorter dash cooldown.
- Paid-only competitive gadget with superior stats.
- Matchmaking advantages.

If gameplay gadgets are introduced, unlock them through fair progression and monetize their cosmetics, not power.

## Economy health metrics

Track:
- Payer conversion.
- ARPDAU / ARPPU.
- Cosmetic attach/equip rate.
- Pass purchase rate.
- Currency earn/spend balance.
- Store impression-to-purchase conversion.
- Time to first cosmetic earned.
- Whether non-payers still feel visually expressive.

## Ethical retention

Avoid:
- Excessive countdown pressure.
- Misleading “discounts.”
- Loot boxes with opaque odds.
- Punitive streak loss designed to sell relief.
- Progression systems that require unhealthy session lengths.

A competitive game needs trust. Long-term social retention is more valuable than short-term coercion.
