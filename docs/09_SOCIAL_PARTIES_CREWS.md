# Social, Parties, Friends, and Crews

## Social goal

The game should turn strangers into rivals, rivals into friends, and friends into recurring parties.

## Result-screen social loop

After a close match, the primary actions should be obvious:
- Rematch.
- Add friend.
- Party up (when appropriate).
- View profile.
- Return to lobby.

Rematch should not be hidden behind menus. If several players accept, prioritize keeping the group together.

## Friends

Features:
- Friend code/link.
- Recent players list.
- Online status with privacy controls.
- Invite to party.
- Challenge to private match.
- One-tap spectate can be considered later.

## Parties

Initial target:
- Up to 4 players in normal party.
- Private rooms can support up to arena capacity.
- Party leader chooses queue.
- Party members can preview each other's cosmetics.

The game should communicate if a party causes harder matchmaking or different queue eligibility.

## Crews

Crews are the clan/guild layer.

Suggested capacity: 20–30 members.

Crew functions:
- Name, tag, badge.
- Member list and roles.
- Weekly collective Energy target.
- Crew leaderboard.
- Friendly challenge.
- Seasonal Crew banner/cosmetics.

MVP can omit Crews entirely. They become valuable after the core friend loop is healthy.

## District War concept

Lore framing: Crews represent city districts competing to power parts of Gridspire.

Weekly structure:
- Normal eligible matches generate Crew Energy up to a personal contribution cap.
- Crews are grouped into comparable divisions.
- End-of-week placement grants cosmetic/profile rewards.
- Avoid requiring 24/7 participation or encouraging unhealthy play hours.

## Communication

MVP recommendation:
- Preset emote wheel.
- Quick team pings in team modes.
- No open voice chat.
- No unrestricted text chat.

Later text/voice features require moderation, reporting, age controls, regional compliance, and significant operational investment.

## Shareable challenges

Useful growth loops:
- Share a friend challenge link.
- Share ranked promotion card.
- Share daily challenge score.
- Share short replay/highlight only after replay tech exists.

Do not make sharing mandatory for progression.

## Social safety

- Mute/block/report available from recent players and match results.
- Emote spam rate limits.
- Blocked players should not receive direct invitations.
- Public profile reveals only intended game identity, never sensitive account data.
